Locations, kits and intros
  • A location defines a card and where the player starts.
  • Each location offers the kits players can choose from.
  • An optional intro plays after arrival.

Saved kits and intros form your library. Reuse them across locations or packs. Editing shared content changes every linked location.

The example opens a new pack with two locations that share one kit and one intro. Your saved work stays in your library.

            1. Extract to get the two ZIPs below.
            2. Put in your world’s datapacks folder.
            3. Put in each player’s resourcepacks folder and enable it.
            4. With Heritage installed, run /reload, then /heritage validate @s as an operator.
