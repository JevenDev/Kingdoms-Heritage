        How to use your pack
        Put the downloaded ZIP in your world’s datapacks folder, then run /reload. Heritage must be installed. Run /heritage validate @s as an operator to check installed items, locations and equipment slots.
        Use a fresh player to test kit grants. /heritage reset @s reopens selection without granting another kit. Visit each destination to check that it can be found in your world.
        Made for Minecraft 1.21.1. If you use items or locations from other mods, install those mods too.
        Your pack replaces Heritage’s default starting locations and Empty-handed kit. To keep them as well, tick the options under Pack options. Other installed datapacks can still add their own choices.
