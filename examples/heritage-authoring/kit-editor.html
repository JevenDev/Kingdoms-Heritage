Shortcuts
Item list: scroll for more items. Click to pick up one. Middle-click or Ctrl-click for a full stack. Shift-left-click fills the selected empty slot. Shift-right-click adds one. Click back into the list to discard.
Slots: left-click moves or swaps. Right-click splits or places one. Drag to spread items. Shift-click transfers. Double-click collects. Middle-click copies a stack. Alt-click inspects.
Selection: Ctrl/Cmd-click selects slots. In selection mode, Shift-click selects a range.
Keys: 1–9 swap with hotbar slots. F swaps with offhand. Delete / Backspace removes. Ctrl+C / V copies and pastes. Ctrl+F searches. Ctrl+Z / Y undoes and redoes.
Escape: cancel a drag or return the held item.
