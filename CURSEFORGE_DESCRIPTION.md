<h2><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/JevenDev/Kingdoms-Heritage/main/images/features.png" width="100%" style="width: 100%;" alt="Features"></h2>
<h3>Choose where you begin</h3>
<p>On your first join, pick world spawn, a village, open plains, or a random spot in the wilderness.</p>
<p><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/JevenDev/Kingdoms-Heritage/main/images/gameplay/starting-locations.png" width="100%" style="width: 100%;" alt="The starting-location screen with Default, Random, Village, and Plains cards"></p>
<h3>Start together</h3>
<p>Invite friends from the selection screen. Your party shares a starting location, and everyone picks their own gear.</p>
<p><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/JevenDev/Kingdoms-Heritage/main/images/gameplay/multiplayer-spongebob.png" width="100%" style="width: 100%;" alt="The multiplayer party screen with SpongeBob in a two-player group"></p>
<h3>Choose your kit</h3>
<p>Choose an <code>Adventurer</code> or <code>Miner</code> kit, pick <code>Naked</code> to start empty-handed, or spend points on tools, food, and armor in the <code>BYO</code> shop. Double-check your inventory and spawn before confirming. A server admin can reopen selection, and you keep your existing inventory on a reset.</p>
<p><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/JevenDev/Kingdoms-Heritage/main/images/gameplay/starter-kit.png" width="100%" style="width: 100%;" alt="The Adventurer starter kit inventory preview with the Miner and BYO kits alongside it"></p>
<p><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/JevenDev/Kingdoms-Heritage/main/images/gameplay/byo-shop.png" width="100%" style="width: 100%;" alt="The BYO starter kit shop with a pickaxe and sword selected and eight points left"></p>
<h3>Wake up in your location</h3>
<p>After confirming your spawn and kit, you’ll arrive at your chosen location with a first-person wake-up animation by default. By default, each player’s chosen starting location also becomes their respawn point. Servers can disable this with <code>homelandRespawn</code>.</p>
<p><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/JevenDev/Kingdoms-Heritage/main/images/gameplay/wake-up-intro.gif" width="100%" style="width: 100%;" alt="A player wakes up in the Plains, stands, and brings their starter sword and shield into view"></p>

<h2><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/JevenDev/Kingdoms-Heritage/main/images/faq.png" width="100%" style="width: 100%;" alt="FAQ"></h2>
<div class="spoiler" data-title="View FAQ">
<h3>Will returning players see the selection screen in an existing world?</h3>
<p>Players who have already played in that world skip selection by default. New players still choose their start/kit.</p>
<h3>Can I spawn with my friends?</h3>
<p>Yes! While everyone is still on the starting-location screen on the same server, open the player list (<code>Tab</code> by default), select a friend under <code>Other players</code>, and choose <code>Invite to party</code>. Once they accept, the leader picks a shared spawn. Everyone chooses a kit and confirms before the party travels.</p>
<h3>Why does it say “Locating...” when choosing a spawn?</h3>
<p>The game is searching for a spot that matches your choice, such as a village or a plains biome. If you see <code>No matching location found within the configured search radius</code>, pick a different starting location. The target may be farther away than the search radius, configured incorrectly in your custom datapack, or missing from the world’s generation settings.</p>
<h3>Can I use custom starting locations or kits?</h3>
<p>Yes, the <a href="https://jevendev.github.io/Kingdoms-Heritage/">Heritage website</a> has examples and pack tools that should help you along the way. I recommend reading the wiki and examples, but feel free to ask questions in <a href="https://discord.gg/3MaX8VQs5j">my Discord server</a>.</p>
<p>To hide the bundled kits or replace them with your own, see the <a href="https://jevendev.github.io/Kingdoms-Heritage/docs.html#/overrides">guide to overriding bundled options</a>. This mod is intended for modpack authors to create their own specialized kits, so I encourage exploring all the features it has to offer. Feel free to let me know if its missing something that would benefit the mod overall or if anything is broken!</p>
